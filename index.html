Good to know

  • Change lines at District Court. It's where the Purple and Aqua lines meet.
  • RTO Pune and Mangalwar Peth are the same stop. Announcements use the RTO name.
  • Pune Railway Station is on the Aqua Line, two stops east of District Court.
  • Swargate connects to the city's biggest bus terminal.
  • Book on your phone through the Pune Metro app or WhatsApp to skip the counter queue.
  • Hours change for festivals. Trains have run late into the night during Ganeshotsav.
